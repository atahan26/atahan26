## Hi, I'm Atahan

I turn manual, repetitive work into small tools that run on their own, more and more with AI agents.
I'm based in Hamburg, finishing an M.A. in Brand Strategy (February 2027) after a B.Sc. in Statistics.
For the last four years I have checked and automated fleet and billing data at a fleet-management company.

## What I build

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**JARVIS**](https://github.com/atahan26/jarvis-local-assistant) | A free, local voice assistant for Windows. Whisper, Qwen3-8B and Kokoro run on my own PC, 11 allowlisted tools control apps, and you can interrupt it mid-sentence (it stops in 22 ms). Measured against its spec, misses included. |
| [**CMK75 menu editor**](https://github.com/atahan26/cmk75-menu-editor) | Turns the screen and knob of a programmable keyboard into a Stream Deck-style launcher, with a browser editor and a screen-recovery tool. English, Turkish, Chinese, Russian and Korean README. |

Most of my other work is private: it is client or employer code.

## How I work with AI

I decide what gets built, design the architecture, test on real hardware and real data, and keep what works.
[Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) writes most of the code, and I say so in every README.
I would rather show a measured result, including the targets I missed, than a polished claim.
